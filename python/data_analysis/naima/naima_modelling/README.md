# Naima Modelling for Gamma-Ray Source Analysis

Fitting hadronic and leptonic cosmic-ray emission models to the gamma-ray spectrum
of the **SGR 1900+14 region** with [naima](https://naima.readthedocs.io),
reproducing and extending **Hnatyk et al. 2022 (MNRAS 516, 4196)** — also a
teaching tutorial for spectral fitting.

➡️ **Everything lives in [`sgr1900+14/`](sgr1900+14/). Start with its full guide:
[`sgr1900+14/README.md`](sgr1900+14/README.md).**

That guide is self-contained and covers:
- what each notebook does (foundations tutorial, hadronic fit, leptonic fit, figures);
- what spectra we use and why (real Fermi + propagated TeV vs the paper's
  equal-weight construction) and how Fermi is connected with H.E.S.S./HAWC;
- **how to plug in your own spectra** (step-by-step, with code);
- the two physical models, how to interpret the results, and reference values.

New to Bayesian/MCMC fitting? Read
[`sgr1900+14/BAYESIAN_PRIMER.md`](sgr1900+14/BAYESIAN_PRIMER.md) first.

The reference paper PDF (Hnatyk et al. 2022) sits in this folder.
